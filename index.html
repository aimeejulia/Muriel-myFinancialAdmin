Guide

How to read this report

Use this panel to quickly understand what the numbers include, what they exclude, and what to do next when the figures change.

How this snapshot works

  • Totals are based on invoice issue date inside the selected quarter or full year.
  • Net invoiced uses invoice subtotals, while paid and outstanding focus on collection status.
  • Income trend compares invoiced value, expenses, and estimated net over time.

What to watch weekly

  • Outstanding receivables should trend down as reminders are sent and payments are booked.
  • VAT exposure should not keep rising for more than one reporting period.
  • Delinquent totals indicate cashflow risk and should trigger follow-up action.

Data and operations notes

  • Aborted invoices are excluded from financial totals and reporting cards.
  • Paid status and payment dates are manual entries unless automation is connected later.
  • CSV exports mirror report cards and can be shared with advisors or imported elsewhere.
